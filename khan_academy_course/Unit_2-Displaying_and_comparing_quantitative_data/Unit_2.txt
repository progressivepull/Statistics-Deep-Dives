Unit 2: Displaying and comparing quantitative data

About this unit

Can you measure it with numbers? Then it’s quantitative data! This unit covers some basic methods for graphing distributions of quantitative data like dot plots, histograms, and stem and leaf plots. We’ll also explore how to use these displays to compare the features of different distributions.
