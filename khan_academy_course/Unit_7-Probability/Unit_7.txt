Unit 7: Probability

About this unit

Probability tells us how often an event will happen after many repeated trials. You’ve experienced probability when you’ve flipped a coin, rolled some dice, or looked at a weather forecast. Go deeper with understanding of probability as you learn about theoretical, experimental, and compound probability, and investigate permutations, combinations, and more!
